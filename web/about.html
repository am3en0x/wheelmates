Squeekchain Genesis Code Lane

Wheelmates

A tap game around Squeektoshi, the real hamster whose wheel powers Squeekchain. You push a wheel for seeds; he sets the tempo. Testnet only: seeds have no value, there is no token and nothing is sold.

How it reads Squeekchain

  • Every 5 seconds the server reads the wheel's status from Squeekchain's public API: whether the device is online, its RPM and speed, today's distance, the Squeek block height and progress.
  • Two readings in a row with the wheel turning make him running: the wheel on your screen turns at his real RPM and every tap pays ×2. At 4.5 km/h or more he has the zoomies: ×3. Still or off air: ×1.
  • Every 15 seconds the server reads the event feed. A settlement on SqueekCore that completes Squeek blocks gives every player who was around in the last day a chest.
  • Passive income from the pantry runs ×1.5 while he runs, computed from a log of his phases, so what you earn away from the game is also his doing.
  • If you connect a wallet that holds Squeeks (in the wallet or in staking), the account API gives you a holder bonus: 10% per Squeek-equivalent (a Level 2 counts as 2.2), up to 50%, rechecked every ten minutes.

The wallet signature

Signing in means signing a plain text message with your wallet: no gas, no transaction, no chain switch, no approval. It only proves the address is yours, so your seeds, cage and place on the board belong to it. The game never asks you to send a transaction and never touches a token.

What the game never does

  • No transactions, no token, no payouts, no purchases. Seeds are points.
  • No tracking beyond what the game needs: your address, your seeds and your upgrades.
  • Not affiliated with Squeekchain. A community build for the Genesis Code Lane.

Fair play

Everything is counted on the server: energy, seeds, multipliers and chests. Your browser only reports how many times you tapped, at most 15 a second and never beyond your energy, which comes back one point a second. Autoclickers gain nothing a thumb cannot.
